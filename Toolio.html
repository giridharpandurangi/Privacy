Privacy Policy — Toolio

Last updated: June 2026

Toolio ("the app") is designed to respect your privacy completely. This policy explains what data the app does and does not handle.

Data We Collect

We collect nothing. Toolio does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. We have no servers, no analytics, no tracking, and no advertising.

Data Stored on Your Device

All your calculations, history, favorites, and settings are stored locally on your device only. This data never leaves your phone. If you uninstall the app, this data is deleted.

Internet Access

Toolio works fully offline. The only optional feature that uses the internet is the Currency Converter, which fetches exchange rates from a public source (frankfurter.app). You can disable all internet access using Offline Mode in Settings. No personal data is ever sent during this process.

Permissions

The app may request the following permissions, used only for the stated feature, and never for data collection:

  • Camera — only for the QR Scanner and Magnifier tools.
  • Location — only for the Compass tool (Android requires location permission for compass/magnetometer access). Your location is never recorded or transmitted.
  • Notifications — only to alert you when a Timer finishes.

Purchases

Toolio offers a one-time in-app purchase to unlock all tools. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment information.

Children's Privacy

Toolio does not collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL.
